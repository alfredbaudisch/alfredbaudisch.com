-   My father, Reinold Baudisch, was one of the most famous and important anglers in Brazil. For more than 40 years he founded different projects, all related to fishing: stores, newspapers, magazines, radio talk show, courses, books and a TV fishing show (his memorial/museum website is coming soon).
-   [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/pescadinamica).

-   From 2001 to 2019 he was the owner, host and producer of Pesca Dinâmica, a TV fishing show that aired in the Brazilian open TV. By 2019 the show had close to 2000 episodes.
-   His legacy and most of his videos are live on the [Pesca Dinâmica YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/pescadinamica).
-   Since [his passing in 2019](https://alfredbaudisch.medium.com/reinold-baudisch-meu-papai-meu-her%C3%B3i-e-professor-dos-pescadores-brasileiros-1c92374aad75), **I've been recording and publishing fishing videos to continue his legacy.**
-   My wife is also helping by editing and making compilations and re-editings of his previous videos.

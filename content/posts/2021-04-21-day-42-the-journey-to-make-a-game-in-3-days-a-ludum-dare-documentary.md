April last year, I participated in the game jam [Ludum Dare](https://ldjam.com), where [I created a game](https://alfredbaudisch.com/projects/games/) from scratch in 72 hours. Everything was created from zero by me: art, coding, sounds, and more.

I recorded everything and now, one year later, 3 days before the next Ludum Dare, I edited and published a mini-documentary showing my journey from last year.

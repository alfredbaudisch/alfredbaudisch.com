F[ire Alpaca](https://firealpaca.com/) is a tiny and free painting software. It has only the basics, but it’s surprisingly good at it.

And it’s fast, very fast. And also lightweight, very lightweight. It opens in a few milliseconds.

But line quality is very bad using my [XP Pen Artist 24 Pro](https://criajogo.com/tela-gigantesca-para-arte-digital-xp-pen-artist-24-pro-review-e-unboxing/) drawing monitor. I got annoyed by it – even though I don’t want to draw, I just want to paint.

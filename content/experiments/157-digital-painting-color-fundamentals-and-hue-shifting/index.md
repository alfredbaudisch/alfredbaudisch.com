Learning color harmony and hue-shifting, or how to pick up lighter or darker colors without just going to darker or lighter pigments, but instead, by shifting the hue.

### Hue Shifting

To **pick up a lighter color** relative to your current color: increase brightness, and then slide to a **WARMER HUE**:

### Practicing Digital Painting Tools and Hue Shifting

-   Paint RGB and CMYK cubes
-   Learn complimentaries
-   Learn hard and soft brushes, as well blending/mixing
